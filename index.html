Why altitude matters

Ceylon tea grows across seven regions, from warm southern hills to gardens above two thousand metres. Higher up, cold nights slow the leaf down, and a slower leaf makes a brighter, more fragrant cup.

Altitude0 m
The seven regions

Climb from sea level to the cloud line.

Each region gives the leaf a different character. Low-grown teas are dark and malty. The higher you go, the lighter and more floral the cup becomes.
